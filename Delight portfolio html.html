About Me — The Protector Behind the Screen

I am Delight Ekeanya, a B.Sc. Cyber Security student at MIVA Open University, based in Lagos, Nigeria. I didn't stumble into Cyber Security — I chose it, because I believe security is not just about systems, it's about people.


In a country where digital innovation is growing faster than digital protection, I saw a gap that needs passionate defenders. Every phishing email that empties a small business, every data breach that exposes families — these are not just technical failures, they are human stories. That is why I am here.


"I love my career because it allows me to be a quiet guardian — the one who watches, learns, and stops the attack before the damage is done."

What drives me: I am fascinated by the mindset of both attacker and defender. I love dissecting how threats work — not to cause harm, but to understand how to build stronger defenses. From analyzing suspicious email headers to monitoring network traffic in a virtual lab, I find purpose in the details others overlook.


My Core Strengths:
• Threat Analysis & SOC Fundamentals — Monitoring, detection, and incident triage
• Network Security — Firewalls, OSI Model, TCP/IP, Subnetting
• Ethical Hacking Mindset — Think like an attacker, act like a protector
• Web Security — HTML, CSS, Secure Coding Practices
• Python for Security — Automation and log analysis
• Soft Skills — Critical thinking, clear documentation, calm under pressure


My Vision: To become a certified SOC Analyst (Security+ & CEH certified) and work in a Security Operations Center where I can protect organizations, mentor other young women in tech, and contribute to a more resilient digital Nigeria.


Beyond the screen, I am a lifelong learner, a builder, and someone who believes that with discipline and curiosity, you can secure not just networks, but futures.

Study Planner — Discipline Creates Freedom

Cyber Security rewards consistency. My weekly structure balances deep theory, hands-on labs, and rest — because a sharp analyst needs both focus and balance.

Monday COS 102 & Computing Lab — Practical sessions, documentation, and peer review. 2-3 hours focused lab time.
Tuesday Network Fundamentals — OSI, TCP/IP, Wireshark basics, subnetting exercises. Building the map of how data travels.
Wednesday Security Theory — CIA Triad, risk assessment, vulnerability management. Reading research papers and writing summaries.
Thursday Ethical Hacking Practice — TryHackMe & safe labs. Learning attacker techniques to strengthen defense. Strictly ethical, strictly lab-contained.
Friday Build Day — Portfolio updates, Python scripts for log parsing, and project documentation. Shipping work.
Saturday Growth Day — Python coding, YouTube deep-dives (SOC workflows), and certification prep (Security+).
Sunday Reset — Family, faith, rest, and light reading. A clear mind detects threats faster.

Daily Rhythm: 6:00 AM Rise & Plan | 7:00-9:00 AM Deep Study | 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM Lectures | 4:00-6:00 PM Hands-On Labs | 8:00-9:00 PM Review & Journal
